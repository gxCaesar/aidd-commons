## Preparation and learning objectives

This laboratory serves `t07-applicability-domain`. Read [applicability-domain diagnostics](/learn/qsar-properties/applicability-domain-diagnostics/) and the [FreeSolv baseline guide](/labs/freesolv-honest-baselines/) first. You will calculate a distance to the fitted population, compare error across distance halves, and explain why the result is a diagnostic rather than a domain boundary. Familiarity with molecular descriptors and ridge regression is useful.

Mode is fresh independent computation over a frozen protocol and the local FreeSolv release. Use the project Python 3.11 environment and CPU. No network, download or accelerator is needed. `check` and `show` write nothing. The exporter overwrites only the committed aggregate summary after the audit passes.

```bash
.venv/bin/python labs/applicability_domain_audit/run_lab.py check
.venv/bin/python labs/applicability_domain_audit/inspect_applicability_domain_audit.py show
.venv/bin/python labs/applicability_domain_audit/inspect_applicability_domain_audit.py check
.venv/bin/python labs/applicability_domain_audit/export_summary.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest labs/tests/applicability_domain_audit -q
```

## Distance and error contrast

The distance representation is a laboratory choice of five RDKit properties, MolWt, MolLogP, TPSA, NumHDonors and NumHAcceptors. For each property, an absolute difference is divided by its training-set population standard deviation, with 1 used if that deviation is zero. The mean of those five scaled differences is calculated for each test–training pair. The smallest pair distance is the test molecule's nearest-training distance. Ridge regression on chirality-aware Morgan fingerprints supplies predictions. Its `alpha=1.0`, radius 2 and 2048 bits are fixed before this computation.

The median and quartiles below summarize the 128 nearest-training distances. These distances have no physical unit. The test molecule with the largest nearest-training distance is identified by its source ID. ## Limits of the material

The larger-distance half has a higher median error in this run. That contrast does not identify a usable threshold or estimate the error of a particular new molecule. Distinct structures can share nearly identical values of these five properties. The audit does not fit a better model or turn its chosen distance into a claim about chemical mechanism. This scaffold split probes held-out grouping under one operational scaffold definition. A random split can leave related scaffolds on both sides and supports a different generalization claim.
