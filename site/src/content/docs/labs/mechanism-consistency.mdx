## Competing explanations and discriminator

The mediator-specific explanation says the transcriptional change follows the compound acting through its intended target. The generic-stress explanation says a perturbing compound can produce a similar change regardless of its target. For each compound admitted in both MCF7 and PC3, the owning reader supplies vehicle-corrected profiles over 978 measured landmark genes at 10.0 um and 24.0 h. The matched Spearman rho compares that compound across the two lines. The mismatched rho compares its MCF7 profile with the next compound's PC3 profile in a fixed sorted-ID rotation by 1.

The declared informative threshold is matched rho 0.1. A lower matched value leaves the test undecided, even if the mismatched value is high. When the matched value reaches 0.1, a strictly higher mismatched value puts the pattern on the generic-stress side. An informative mismatched value at or below the matched value also leaves the case undecided because another compound agrees too. The mediator-specific side requires matched rho at least 0.1 and mismatched rho below 0.1. No undecided case counts as support.

## Falsifying observation and independent task

The checkable falsifying quantity is `generic_stress_count`. It counts compounds with matched rho at least 0.1 whose mismatched rho is strictly higher. The observed value is 123. These cases run against the mediator-specific pattern under this fixed control. They do not establish generic stress as the cause.

Inspect a compound assigned `informative_mismatched_control` in the `show` output. Explain why its matched correlation alone would overstate the mechanism case. Then choose a target-proximal perturbation or rescue whose predictions would differ between the explanations. Compare that proposal with the [lesson worked case](/learn/mechanism-targets/perturbation-to-target-validation/).

## Material limits and sources

This comparison can challenge a target-specific response pattern against one different-compound control. It cannot identify the intended target, establish a disease mechanism, or show that a compound is worth making. The rotated control is not a matched stress-only perturbation. Disease-relevant context and an orthogonal target-proximal measurement remain necessary for a causal claim. No reader outcome has been observed.
