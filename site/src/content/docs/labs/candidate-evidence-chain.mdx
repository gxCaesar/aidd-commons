## Preparation and learning objectives

Read the [candidate nomination workbook](/learn/candidate-nomination/capstone-workbook/) and the [multi-endpoint profile guide](/labs/multi-endpoint-integration/). This laboratory asks what a single compound's evidence can support when the links from mechanism to reproducibility are kept in order. You will identify each link's source and state, find the first link outside measured evidence, and keep later measurements visible without promoting them into a nomination claim.

## Fresh independent computation

The mode is a fresh independent computation under a frozen ChEMBL 37 protocol. Use the pinned Python 3.11 environment and the existing cached snapshots. The commands need no network after caching and no accelerator. `download` validates those caches without writing. `show` prints each link with its measurements. `check` recomputes the chain and checks the committed summary. The export overwrites only that small summary after the audits pass.

```bash
.venv/bin/python labs/candidate_evidence_chain/run_lab.py download
.venv/bin/python labs/candidate_evidence_chain/inspect_candidate_evidence_chain.py show
.venv/bin/python labs/candidate_evidence_chain/inspect_candidate_evidence_chain.py check
.venv/bin/python labs/candidate_evidence_chain/export_summary.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest labs/tests/candidate_evidence_chain -q
```

## Evidence states and eight links

A measured link has an admitted value for this parent, its unit, assay or target, and document. An absent link has no qualifying record in a family measured elsewhere. A constructed link rests only on teaching data. A not-covered link has no compound-matched material here. Constructed teaching cases are not attached to these ChEMBL parents, so no chain below uses one as evidence.

| Link in order | Rule in this laboratory | State across the 3 parents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism | Requires compound-specific mechanism validation | not covered in 3 |
| Target | Requires measured intended-target engagement or validation | not covered in 3 |
| Activity | Admitted exact `AC50`, `EC50` or `IC50` response-potency point | measured in 1, absent in 2 |
| Selectivity | One admitted CYP3A4 and one CYP2D6 `IC50` from the same document, each based on one activity | measured in 1, absent in 2 |
| Exposure | Admitted intrinsic clearance in a named in vitro assay system | measured in 3 |
| Safety | No compound-matched safety measurement | not covered in 3 |
| Synthesis | No compound-matched experimental synthesis result | not covered in 3 |
| Reproducibility | No independent replication of the whole compound chain | not covered in 3 |

The CYP link describes only differential inhibition of CYP3A4 and CYP2D6. It does not establish selectivity against an intended therapeutic target. The clearance link reports a real in vitro measurement, but it cannot establish systemic exposure. The safety and route-recovery teaching laboratories use constructed data for other objects. Their records cannot fill either link for these parents.

Families that this repository does not measure for these compounds are named as not covered rather than filled with another compound's values.

## Claim boundary and independent exercise

A chain supports a claim only when every link is measured. An absent, constructed or not-covered link stops it. Even a fully measured chain could carry no claim broader than the first measured link's own scope. This laboratory does not establish a development candidate, a correct mechanism, or a route to fill the gaps. It contains no measured safety, synthesis or clinical outcome for these parents. Running the audit again is not independent experimental replication.

Inspect `CHEMBL1963249`. Explain why its same-document CYP pair is a measured selectivity link with narrow scope, and why it still supports no nomination claim.

<details data-worked-answer>
<summary>Worked answer</summary>

The two CYP measurements describe differential inhibition of those enzymes in one document. They do not test intended-target selectivity. The chain stops at mechanism before reaching that pair. The safety, synthesis and reproducibility links are also not covered. No claim crosses those gaps.

</details>
