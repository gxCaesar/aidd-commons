## Before you run

**Mode: runnable comparison over committed files.** The command reads two JSON
files and applies reaction SMARTS with RDKit. **No model is trained or executed**,
no reaction is run, and nothing here is a synthesis proposal.

```bash
python labs/retro_disconnection/inspect_disconnection.py compare
```

Four disconnection families are usually compared with one recovery percentage.
That number hides two things: the families fail for different reasons, and
recovering a recorded route says nothing about whether the route can be run.

## 1. Three families run, and one says why it does not

`rule` transforms are written by hand from chemical knowledge. `template`
transforms have a dataset as their provenance rather than a person. `retrieval`
looks the product up and returns what was recorded against it. `learned` is
**declared without being run**, because no model is executed anywhere in these
offline labs.

That last line is the point of listing it. A comparison that quietly drops a
family implies four were compared when three were, and the boundary should be
printed rather than inferred.

## 2. All three are perfect inside the precedent set

The precedent set is both what retrieval may look up and what the templates were
extracted from. Five of the ten routes are in it.

Inside it, every family recovers 5 of 5. **Outside it they separate completely:**
rules recover 5 of 5, templates 3 of 5, retrieval 0 of 5.

The separation comes from one deliberate choice in the constructed set: **the
precedent set contains no suzuki-biaryl reaction.** Retrieval misses those
molecules because they are not in its table. Templates miss them because no
template exists for a class the dataset never held. Hand-written rules cover the
class because someone chose to write it.

**Same score column, three different causes.** A percentage cannot tell you which
one you are looking at, and the repair for each is different: collect different
data, extract from a broader dataset, or write another rule.

## 3. The same method, two scores, one split

Retrieval recovers 5 of 5 routes that are in the precedent set. That is **100%**,
and it is a lookup succeeding on its own table. Over every route it recovers 5 of
10, **50%**.

Neither number is wrong. Which one gets reported is decided entirely by the
split, and **the method that memorises is the one most rewarded by choosing
badly.** A retrieval baseline evaluated on molecules it has already seen will
beat everything and generalise to nothing.

## 4. Recovering a route is not being right

On three of the ten products the rules propose **more than one** disconnection.
Reductive amination admits two, and so does one of the biaryls.

Recovery means the recorded route was **among** the proposals. It does not mean
the method selected it, ranked it first, or preferred it to the alternative. A
family that proposes many disconnections buys recovery cheaply, and a comparison
that counts only recovery will reward it for that.

```bash
python labs/retro_disconnection/inspect_disconnection.py check
```

Thirteen checks run. A structure that does not parse stops the run before any
family is applied, because a family cannot be compared on a set that does not
read.

## 5. What no score above establishes

The route set records `laboratory_feasibility: not_assessed`. Nothing in this lab
examines reagent availability, protecting groups, condition compatibility,
selectivity, scale or cost.

**Route recovery is a statement about a graph edit. Feasibility is a statement
about a laboratory.** A disconnection that recovers the recorded route can still
need a reagent nobody sells, a protecting group nobody added, or conditions that
destroy the rest of the molecule. This is the same boundary the
[generation gate](/labs/generation-gate/) records when it calls its structural
filters a proxy and keeps `route_feasibility` unassessed.

## Independent exercise

A retrosynthesis paper reports: "our model achieves 92% top-1 route recovery on
USPTO-50K, outperforming template-based methods at 87%. This demonstrates that
learned disconnections are ready for production planning."

Name what the comparison does not establish, and say which single number you
would ask for first.

<details data-worked-answer>
<summary>Worked answer — three gaps, and the number to ask for</summary>

**"Route recovery" is not correctness.** It measures agreement with one recorded
route. A chemist proposing a different and better disconnection scores zero, and
a model proposing many disconnections that happen to include the record scores
one. Top-1 controls for the second of those, which is why it is the right thing
to report, but it still leaves the first: the target is a single literature
precedent, not a set of acceptable answers.

**The two numbers may not be measuring the same difficulty.** Template methods
inherit the coverage of the dataset the templates came from, and here that is the
same dataset the model trained on. If the test split shares transformation
classes with training, both methods are being asked an easier question than
production planning poses, and the five-point gap is between two scores on that
easier question.

**Nothing here touches production.** Production planning needs available
reagents, compatible conditions, acceptable selectivity, and a route that survives
scale. A recovery percentage is silent on all of them, and the claim in the last
sentence is a different kind of claim from the evidence in the first.

**And the retrieval baseline is missing.** If a nearest-neighbour lookup over the
training set scores anywhere near 87%, most of what both methods achieved is
memorisation, and the comparison between them is measuring something small on top
of something large.

**The number to ask for first: the score on products whose transformation class
does not appear in training.** It separates the three causes that a single
percentage collapses, it exposes how much of the headline is retrieval, and it is
the only one of these numbers that speaks to whether the method would help on a
target nobody has made before. If that number is not available, the honest reading
of 92% is that it describes the dataset.

</details>

## What this does not do

This route does not run a reaction, evaluate a real retrosynthesis model, or
establish that any route is makeable. The ten routes were authored for teaching,
so the counts teach the structure of the comparison rather than the behaviour of
any published method.
