## Preparation and learning objectives

This lab belongs to `t13-sequence-structure-function-objective` and accompanies the [lesson on construct identity and objectives](/learn/biologics-design/construct-identity-and-objectives/). Its question is which public record, if any, carries evidence for each objective. After the lab, you should be able to name the relevant record, interpret its scope, and explain why an empty cell cannot be filled by evidence from another objective. Familiarity with accessions, deposited structures, and tabular records is sufficient preparation. The [canonical English guide](/labs/objective-ladder/) is this page.

Mode is fresh independent computation under the frozen protocol. The reading uses the project Python environment and CPU. The first download needs network access. Later steps read the cache and require no accelerator. The protocol fixes the examined entities, objectives, source routes, and coordinate-audit tolerances before the records are read.

## Evidence at each objective

### Sequence recovery

The structure archive's alignment identifies the deposited polymer region and its UniProtKB reference. The lab compares their sequences position by position over that aligned region. A match fraction does not describe positions outside the alignment or establish recovery of a whole accession.

### Folding

A deposited experimental structure records a conformation in the state produced by the experiment. The model archive also holds a prediction for each accession. A predicted model is a computational output, not an observation of folding, and neither record establishes that the conformation applies in another state.

### Structural plausibility

The lab audits deposited coordinate rows using a 2.0 angstrom clash distance and a 0.45 angstrom bond-length tolerance. It audits an entry only when at least 500 coordinate rows are present. The rows include the examined entity, partners, ligands, and water. Acceptable geometry describes internal consistency of that coordinate file. It does not show that the fold depicted is correct.

### Binding

A SKEMPI 2.0 row supplies a measured affinity value only when its structure is linked to the examined accession by the structure archive itself. A component in a deposited structure cannot substitute for that value. Crystallisation additives and ions occur in deposited entries, so component presence alone is not a measured affinity.

### Catalysis

The UniProtKB accession can carry a catalytic-activity annotation with a named reaction and catalogue number. The annotation identifies what the source states. It is not a measured rate. Annotation count and distinct catalogue-number count are separate quantities.

### Function

The UniProtKB accession can carry a functional annotation. Its presence records an attributed statement about function, not a direct measurement of sequence recovery, folding, geometry, binding, or catalysis.

## Reading of public records

The committed reading was computed on 2026-10-22 from records read on 2026-10-22 with Python 3.11.15, numpy 2.4.6, and scipy 1.17.1. Sequence cells give matching and evaluated positions in the archive-aligned region. Folding cells name the experimental structure, its reference coverage, and the model entry with its global metric. All model entries are dated 2025-08-01 and remain predictions. Geometry cells give all audited rows, rows of the examined entity, bond-length outliers, and clashes in that order. The geometry counts are computed from coordinates, not copied from an archive score. Binding cells give the measured wild-type and mutant values in M and the assay method. Catalysis cells retain the named reaction as written by the archive. Function cells identify the accession's annotation without turning its wording into another measurement.

## Empty cells

DHFR has no binding record under `no-affinity-record`. No affinity-archive row names a structure of this target, and its declared structure 1BOZ has no bound component. ESR1 also has no measured affinity record, although 11SD carries A1DAI. ABCD1 likewise has no measured affinity record, although 6C0V carries ATP and MG. These components do not turn either empty cell into a measurement. TP53 and ESR1 have empty catalysis cells under `no-catalysis-annotation` because their accessions carry neither a catalytic-activity annotation nor an enzyme catalogue number. An empty cell describes this reading of the named sources, not a proof that no such evidence exists anywhere.

## Reproduce the reading

The download gathers the declared public records once and checks the affinity snapshot's byte length before it is read. Its cache is reused on later runs. The inspector first displays each objective and its record or absence reason, then checks a fresh reading against the committed summary. The export writes that summary after a successful reading and overwrites the existing summary. The final command checks the laboratory's contract and analysis. The affinity snapshot resides in the cache's `skempi` subdirectory. Run the commands from the repository root in this order.

```bash
.venv/bin/python labs/objective_ladder/run_lab.py download
.venv/bin/python labs/objective_ladder/inspect_objective_ladder.py show
.venv/bin/python labs/objective_ladder/inspect_objective_ladder.py check
.venv/bin/python labs/objective_ladder/export_summary.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest labs/tests/objective_ladder -q
```

The public LiteFold mirror is 6148969 bytes and contains 7085 rows. Its byte length is checked on every run. The download needs network access once. The remaining commands read the local cache.

## What the material does not show

This is a dated reading of public records, not a prediction or scoring exercise. The laboratory predicts or scores no value and does not rank the examined entities. Record presence cannot stand in for evidence at another objective. No reader outcome has been observed, so runnable tests do not establish learning. The reading describes records available on 2026-10-22 and does not claim that a later archive query will return the same content.
