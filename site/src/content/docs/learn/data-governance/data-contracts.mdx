## Foundations

A data contract names the producer, version, entities, labels, transformations,
permitted use, split unit, and expected output. Structure sources such as
[RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/) and [PDBe](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/) can
identify structures, while [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/) and
[InterPro](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/) support protein annotation. None of
those homepage observations settle all downstream terms.

## Practical workflow

Choose stable identifiers before joining chemical, protein, pathway, and assay
tables. Retain unmatched entities and exclusion counts. For reaction records,
inspect the [Open Reaction Database schema](https://github.com/open-reaction-database/ord-schema)
instead of treating a reaction string as a complete provenance record.

## Evaluation

Test joins against held-out entities and audit duplicate or near-duplicate
records across partitions. A successful lookup does not establish causal
linkage, licence compatibility, or a valid experimental endpoint.

## Frontier and open questions

Model cards and data cards are useful only when they bind claims to released
assets. The hard question is whether a data recipe can be rerun without hidden
source databases, changing APIs, or unrecorded manual curation.

## What v0.1 does not yet teach

This lesson does not provide legal advice or a universal ontology alignment
service.
