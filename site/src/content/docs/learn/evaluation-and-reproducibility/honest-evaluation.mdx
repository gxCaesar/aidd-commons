## Foundations

Evaluation begins with the unit expected to generalize: molecule, scaffold,
target, protein family, assay, patient, time period, or experimental campaign.
A high score is not informative until the split, labels, leakage controls, and
comparison protocol are explicit.

## Practical workflow

Write a data contract and freeze split construction, model selection, metric,
and uncertainty reporting before model comparison. Use a cheap baseline and
audit duplicate, scaffold, target, and time overlap. [Therapeutics Data
Commons](https://tdcommons.ai/) and [OGB](https://ogb.stanford.edu/) publish
task definitions, but a project still needs to inspect the matched endpoint.

## Evaluation

Report the benchmark version, inputs, labels, splits, selection protocol,
failed runs, and uncertainty over the actual independent unit. For structures,
[PoseBusters](https://github.com/maabuu/posebusters) checks selected physical
plausibility conditions; it does not validate biological efficacy.

## Frontier and open questions

Out-of-distribution evaluation, dataset shift, label noise, and benchmark
leakage remain active problems. [DrugOOD](https://drugood.github.io/) makes
one family of distribution shifts explicit, not every way a deployed decision
can fail.

## What v0.1 does not yet teach

v0.1 does not publish new benchmark scores, assert that any split is leakage
free by default, or certify reproducibility from a repository link alone.
