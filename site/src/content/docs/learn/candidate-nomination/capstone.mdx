## Foundations

Candidate nomination is a decision under uncertainty, not a leaderboard. The
unit is a defined candidate for a defined target, indication, route, and
experimental next step. Evidence must connect disease mechanism, target,
activity, selectivity, exposure, safety, synthesis, and governance boundaries.

## Practical workflow

Use the [target-to-candidate map](/atlas/target-to-candidate-map/) to make the
decision unit, evidence, AI task, experimental feedback, and failure gate
visible at every stage. Record which claims are supported, unverified, or
outside the available evidence before a project lead decides whether to
advance.

## Evaluation

Require a predeclared evidence package and an independent experimental check
for the next decision. Include counterevidence, failed candidates, assay
limits, uncertainty, and reproducible source references. A structural,
property, or generative score alone cannot nominate a candidate.

## Frontier and open questions

Products such as [Chai-2](https://www.chaidiscovery.com/) and
[IsoDDE](https://isomorphiclabs.com/) may inform a bounded workflow, but their
asset availability and experimental relevance must remain explicit. Open
science and biosecurity constraints can change whether a technically plausible
workflow is appropriate.

## What v0.1 does not yet teach

v0.1 does not nominate a clinical candidate, provide a safety authorization,
or replace project-specific medicinal chemistry, pharmacology, and governance
review.

## Research decision practice

Continue to [From computational evidence to a validation decision](/learn/candidate-nomination/evidence-to-validation/)
to compare alternative explanations, work through a measurement counterexample,
and write a next-observation decision memo. The case is constructed; it does not
nominate a real compound or supply an experimental procedure.

The [five-case capstone workbook](/learn/candidate-nomination/capstone-workbook/)
now supplies complete target, property, structure, experiment-allocation and
nomination cases, each with a worked answer and transfer question. Produce five
short memos and a synthesis using its stated rubric. All case values are
constructed; the workbook is not five newly run experiments.

## Core-source path

Read the [Open Science in Drug Discovery principles](https://opensciencedrugdiscovery.org/)
and [SPDX](https://spdx.dev/) materials alongside the
[target-to-candidate map](/atlas/target-to-candidate-map/) before preparing a
nomination package.
