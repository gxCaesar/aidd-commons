## Features are not the experimental object

Read [biological representations](/learn/representations/biological-representations/),
[neural representations](/learn/machine-learning/neural-representations/) and
[entity resolution](/learn/data-governance/identity-lineage-and-reuse/).
This lesson teaches four encodings by hand. You will track their dimensions,
identify which transformations should preserve a prediction, and find information
that the encoding cannot recover. Numerical examples are constructed, not model outputs.

A useful representation makes relevant variation available while suppressing
irrelevant variation. That choice is task-dependent. Translating an isolated
complex should not change its predicted binding energy. Removing the ligand's
orientation relative to the pocket destroys potentially relevant information.
Collapsing every cell from a donor to one vector can stabilize a population
summary while erasing rare cell states. No embedding is sufficient for every task.

## Protein sequences — token, residue and pooled feature

For a protein of length `L`, a residue encoder can return an `L×d` matrix of
context-dependent features. A sequence-level model may pool this to one
`d`-dimensional vector; a residue-level model must preserve the relevant positions.
The [original ESM repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm) illustrates
per-residue extraction and sequence averaging that excludes special tokens.
Its example is a concrete API pattern, not a claim that mean pooling is universally
optimal or that this archived repository is the newest model.

Construct residue features for a three-residue sequence:

```text
residue 1: [1, 0]
residue 2: [0, 2]
residue 3: [2, 1]
padding:   [0, 0]
```

The masked residue mean is `[1,1]`. Averaging over the padded four-row tensor
instead gives `[0.75,0.75]`, making the feature depend on batch padding. Special
start/end tokens also need the encoder's explicit treatment; they are not residues.
If position 2 is a catalytic site, the mean alone does not retain a labelled
feature for that site. Preserve per-residue features or a justified site-specific
pool when the prediction depends on position.

Tokenization is a mapping from the submitted sequence to model inputs, not
biological identity resolution. Verify chain boundaries, unknown residues,
truncation and numbering. A mutation in a full-length reference must be mapped
to the assayed construct before selecting a residue embedding. A pooled vector
for the full protein cannot silently stand in for every isoform, cleavage product
or post-translational state. Likewise, a pretrained embedding is a feature;
its large dimension does not create more independent training proteins.

Alignment changes position correspondence, not just sequence length. In the toy
alignment `AC-D` against `ACED`, the first sequence's residue 3 (D) corresponds
to alignment column 4; column 3 is a gap, not a measured residue. A surface-point
encoding instead samples an accessible boundary under a chosen geometric rule,
while an ensemble encoding represents several states with an explicit weighting
assumption. A surface is not a sequence alignment, and ten model-generated
conformers are not ten experimentally measured state populations. Preserve the
mapping between these objects whenever features are joined.

## Complex graphs — covalent edges and spatial contacts

A ligand–protein graph can contain atoms or residues as nodes. Covalent edges
encode bonds; spatial edges encode proximity in a specified pose. Keep their
types distinct. A distance edge does not establish a hydrogen bond, favorable
interaction or experimentally supported binding event. Protonation, geometry,
solvent and competing states can matter beyond proximity.

Consider two ligand atoms L1 and L2 and two pocket nodes P1 and P2. Their invented
cross-distances in ångströms are:

| Distance, Å | P1  | P2  |
| ----------- | --- | --- |
| L1          | 3.0 | 6.0 |
| L2          | 4.5 | 5.5 |

Define a contact as distance **at most 5.0 Å**. The ligand-to-pocket adjacency
matrix is `[[1,0],[1,0]]`. At a 6.0 Å cutoff it becomes `[[1,1],[1,1]]`.
The model did not discover new interactions; the representation rule changed.
Continuous radial features can avoid this particular hard discontinuity, but
they still need a distance range, scale and neighborhood convention.

Rotating both ligand and protein together preserves these distances. Rotating
only the ligand can change them and represents a different pose. A model using
only pairwise distances also cannot distinguish a configuration from its full
mirror image. That may be inappropriate where chirality matters; choose features
and symmetry assumptions consistent with the intended chemical task. A graph
without water or a required cofactor cannot learn that omitted object's position
from the graph alone, although it may exploit correlations in its training set.

## Cell states — a matrix with biological context

An expression representation often starts as cells by genes. The
[AnnData documentation](https://anndata.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) describes an
annotated matrix container: observations and variables need aligned annotations,
and additional representations can be stored separately. The container does not
decide whether a row is an independent biological replicate.

A cell-state prediction input should distinguish pre-perturbation context from
the intervention: cell type or state, donor, condition, perturbation identity,
dose and observation time when available. Encoding the perturbation as a token
is useful only if its meaning and availability are defined. A random integer
assigned to each gene should not imply that gene 20 is twice gene 10. Categorical
embeddings learn relationships; sequence or pathway features add a different prior.

Construct two cells with gene G count 100 in each, but total counts 1,000 and
2,000. Total-count normalization to 10,000 yields G values 1,000 and 500.
The normalized value halves even though the recorded count of G is unchanged.
This calculation demonstrates dependence on the denominator, not evidence that
G's absolute biological expression halved. Capture efficiency and composition
also affect interpretation. [Scanpy's normalization documentation](https://scanpy.readthedocs.io/en/stable/generated/scanpy.pp.normalize_total.html)
defines this rescaling and options affecting the size factor.

A gene order mismatch is equally consequential: vectors `[G1,G2]` and `[G2,G1]`
cannot be compared positionwise without reindexing. Keep raw counts, transformed
values and learned latent features distinguishable. Fitting feature selection
or a supervised state encoder using held-out responses exposes evaluation
information even when the final predictor never sees those response columns.

For an unseen-donor claim, thousands of cells from two donors still supply only
two donor units. For an unseen-perturbation claim, holdout identity and context
must be specified together. A control-mean or context-only predictor is a useful
test of how much the endpoint can be predicted without intervention identity;
it is not a universal null for every biological hypothesis.

## Knowledge graphs — relations with evidence

[RDF 1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf11-concepts/) represents statements as
subject–predicate–object triples. For a scientific graph, the predicate must
retain its meaning. `compound inhibits protein`, `gene associated_with disease`
and `paper mentions gene` are different relations, even when each becomes an edge.

Construct a record: compound C inhibits protein P in assay A at context X, reported
by source S. A bare `(C, inhibits, P)` triple loses assay and context. An explicit
evidence node or qualified relation can retain the endpoint, source, direction,
date and uncertainty. The [PROV-O vocabulary](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) is
one way to describe derivation; the graph format alone does not make the claim true.

Suppose two websites repeat the same assay publication. Two edges are not two
independent experiments. Suppose a held-out edge `(C, inhibits, P)` has its exact
inverse `(P, inhibited_by, C)` in the training graph. Predicting the former can
be a reconstruction shortcut, not discovery. Group reciprocal and duplicated
evidence before splitting. Also freeze temporal availability: a graph assembled
after the claimed discovery date can encode the answer through later literature.

An absent edge is usually unknown, not an experimentally confirmed negative.
Sampling absent pairs for link-prediction training is a modelling convention.
Report that convention and evaluate whether its easy examples resemble the real
candidate population. Evidence edges, entity features and training targets should
have separate roles so the answer is not accidentally supplied as an input.

## Build them from deposited complexes

The [EGFR structure evidence lab](/labs/egfr-structure-evidence/) builds contact,
interaction and fingerprint representations from three deposited complexes. A 3.5 A
polar contact becomes a possible hydrogen bond only under declared protonation roles,
and the same ligand in two receptor states shares 65 percent of its
residue-interaction bits.
