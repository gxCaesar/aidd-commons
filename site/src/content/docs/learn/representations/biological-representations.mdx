## Foundations

SMILES, molecular graphs, conformers, sequences, structures, and interaction
graphs encode different objects and discard different information. The
[PCQM4Mv2](https://github.com/snap-stanford/ogb) and
[GEOM](https://github.com/learningmatter-mit/geom) records illustrate why a
property target and a conformational ensemble are not interchangeable inputs.

## Practical workflow

Start by asking what the measurement can depend on. Generate a simple 2D
fingerprint or descriptor baseline, then compare it with a 3D or sequence model
under unchanged data and split rules. Preserve conformer-generation and
protonation choices as part of the input contract.

Continue with the dedicated
[small-molecule representations](/learn/representations/small-molecule-representations/)
chapter for a decision matrix, frozen data contract, and label-free executable
inspector.

Then work through [protein, complex, cell-state and knowledge-graph representations](/learn/representations/complex-cell-and-knowledge-graphs/)
for masked sequence pooling, pose-dependent contact graphs, count normalization,
and evidence-bearing graph relations. It contains complete constructed examples
and independent exercises; it is not a new representation benchmark.

## Evaluation

Inspect whether a representation accidentally carries target, scaffold, or
time information across the split. A representation's dimensionality or
pretraining scale is not an independent evaluation unit.

## Frontier and open questions

Protein language models such as [ESM](https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm),
[ESM3](https://github.com/Biohub/esm), and
[ProtTrans](https://github.com/agemagician/ProtTrans) create useful transfer
questions, but public repository visibility does not settle weight or training
data terms.

## What this introduction does not yet teach

This overview does not benchmark every representation under one matched task
protocol. The dedicated small-molecule chapter defines the first v0.2b depth
tranche without claiming an unrun performance result.

## Core-source path

Inspect [Datamol](https://github.com/datamol-io/datamol),
[MolFeat](https://github.com/datamol-io/molfeat),
[Uni-Mol](https://github.com/deepmodeling/Uni-Mol), and
[Foldseek](https://github.com/steineggerlab/foldseek) as complementary tools.
