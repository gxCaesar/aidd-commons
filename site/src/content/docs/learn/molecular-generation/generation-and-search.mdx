## Foundations

A generated structure is a candidate proposal, not a discovery result. Define
the chemical series, required properties, prohibited motifs, synthetic
constraints, target evidence, and nomination decision before choosing a
generator or an optimizer.

## Practical workflow

Write a frozen multi-objective specification and retain a simple enumerative or
similarity baseline. Use [REINVENT](https://github.com/MolecularAI/REINVENT4)
or another search system only after specifying what each score represents and
which laboratory result will update the next round. Keep novelty, validity,
and synthetic accessibility distinct.

## Evaluation

Use train-only baselines and splits that prevent trivial scaffold or near-
duplicate recovery. [MOSES](https://github.com/molecularsets/moses) and
[GuacaMol](https://github.com/BenevolentAI/guacamol) define benchmark tasks;
they do not establish prospective activity or developability.

## Frontier and open questions

Structure-conditioned systems such as
[Pocket2Mol](https://github.com/pengxingang/Pocket2Mol) and
[TargetDiff](https://github.com/guanjq/targetdiff) raise questions about
target-state uncertainty, pose uncertainty, and experimental feedback. These
questions should be reported before comparing generated lists.

## Choose the generator and evaluator separately

The [generation resource guide](/learn/molecular-generation/generation-resource-guide/)
distinguishes REINVENT 4 from the archived 3.2 implementation, separates
distribution-learning and goal-directed tasks, and works through candidate
validity, uniqueness and novelty denominators.

## What v0.1 does not yet teach

v0.1 does not rank generators, claim that benchmark novelty predicts utility,
or provide a wet-lab validation campaign.

## Core-source path

Read the [REINVENT documentation](https://github.com/MolecularAI/REINVENT4),
then the [MOSES](https://github.com/molecularsets/moses) and
[GuacaMol](https://github.com/BenevolentAI/guacamol) task definitions before
selecting an evaluation.
