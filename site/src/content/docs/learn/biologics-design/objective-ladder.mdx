## Learning goals and prerequisites

This lesson asks which public record, if any, carries evidence for each objective in a sequence-to-function reading. After reading it, you should be able to name a record for an objective, describe its scope, and explain why a record at another objective cannot fill an empty cell. Read [Construct identity and objectives](/learn/biologics-design/construct-identity-and-objectives/) first. Familiarity with accessions, deposited structures and tabular records is sufficient.

The mode is a reading lesson with a runnable laboratory attached. The frozen [objective-ladder lab guide](/labs/objective-ladder/) gives the execution procedure and the committed result that this lesson interprets.

## Evidence at each objective

### Sequence recovery

The structure archive's alignment record identifies a deposited polymer region and its UniProtKB reference. A position-by-position comparison over that region supports a match fraction there. It cannot establish recovery of sequence outside the alignment or of the whole accession.

### Folding

A deposited experimental structure records a conformation in the state produced by the experiment. A model archive entry records a prediction for the accession. The prediction is not an observation of folding, and neither record establishes a conformation in another state.

### Structural plausibility

An audit of deposited coordinate rows can record bond-length outliers and clashes under declared tolerances. Acceptable geometry describes the internal consistency of that coordinate file. It does not show that the fold depicted is correct.

### Binding

A measured affinity row in SKEMPI 2.0 can support this cell when the structure archive itself links the row's structure to the examined accession. A component in a deposited structure is not a measured affinity value.

### Catalysis

A UniProtKB catalytic-activity annotation can state a named transformation and catalogue number. It is a source statement, not a measured rate. The count of annotations and the count of distinct catalogue numbers answer different questions.

### Biological function

A UniProtKB function annotation states what the accession is attributed to do. Its presence is not a measurement of sequence recovery, folding, coordinate geometry, binding or catalysis.

## How the reading is made

The laboratory compares deposited sequence with the named reference only over the region covered by the archive's own alignment record. It audits deposited coordinates under the guide's declared 2.0 angstrom clash distance, 0.45 angstrom bond-length tolerance and minimum of 500 coordinate rows. The rows include the examined entity and other material in the entry. It then reads the affinity archive and checks annotations on the accession. A candidate structure is treated as a structure of the examined entity only when the entry itself lists that entity's accession.

The committed reading was computed on 2026-10-22 from records read on 2026-10-22 with Python 3.11.15, numpy 2.4.6 and scipy 1.17.1. The geometry counts below were computed from coordinates. The model entries are dated 2025-08-01 and remain predictions. The [lab guide](/labs/objective-ladder/) carries the full record wording and procedure.

Geometry entries give all audited rows, rows of the examined entity, bond-length outliers and clashes, in that order. Binding values are measured wild-type and mutant values in M, with the assay method. `None` gives the source-specific absence reason.

## The empty cells

DHFR's binding cell is empty because the affinity archive has no row naming a structure of that target, and its declared structure 1BOZ has no bound component. ESR1's binding cell remains empty despite A1DAI in 11SD. ABCD1's binding cell remains empty despite ATP and MG in 6C0V. Neither component presence supplies a measured affinity. TP53 and ESR1 have no catalytic-activity annotation or enzyme catalogue number on their accessions, so their catalysis cells are empty. These are five source-specific absences, not proof that evidence is absent everywhere.

## Worked case

Consider the ESR1 geometry cell. Its audit includes 3845 coordinate rows, but only 93 belong to the examined entity. The recorded 0 clashes describes the audited entry under the declared threshold, including other material in that entry. The corrected conclusion is that this coordinate file passed that clash check. The result does not independently establish that the fold it depicts is correct.

## Independent exercise

A report treats the ATP component in 6C0V as evidence that ABCD1 has a measured binding value. Which record would fill the binding cell, and what does this reading show instead?

<details data-worked-answer>
<summary>Worked answer</summary>

The cell requires a SKEMPI 2.0 row with a measured affinity value whose structure the archive itself links to P08183. No such row was found in the read snapshot. The cell remains empty under `no-affinity-record`. The component in 6C0V is not a measured affinity value.

</details>

## What this material does not show

No reader outcome has been observed. This laboratory predicts or scores no value and does not rank the examined entities. The table is a reading of specified public records on 2026-10-22, not a claim about every source or a later archive state. Presence at one objective cannot stand in for evidence at another.
