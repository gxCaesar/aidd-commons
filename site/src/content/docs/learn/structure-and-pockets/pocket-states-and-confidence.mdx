## Learning goals and prerequisites

You will define a binding pocket as a property of a declared molecular state,
separate a confidence output from evidence of ligand binding, and make a
reproducible decision about whether an open structural asset can be reused.
Read [Structure, pockets, and co-folding](/learn/structure-and-pockets/cofolding-frontier/)
and [Structure, affinity, and what a prediction names](/learn/structure-and-pockets/structure-affinity-mechanisms/)
first. This is a browser and handworked reasoning lesson. It does not run pocket
detection, predict a ligand, or establish that a site is druggable.

## A pocket is defined by a state and a rule

Calling a surface depression a pocket hides several choices. A useful pocket
definition records a coordinate set or model, biological assembly or chain
selection, protonation and ligand assumptions when relevant, the geometric rule,
and the purpose of the call. Different workflows may call a solvent-accessible
volume or ligand-centred residue neighbourhood, so they can disagree at flexible
gates without either being wrong.

Let `S` be a declared protein or complex state, `G` a geometric pocket rule, and
`P(S, G)` the resulting set of atoms, residues, or volume. The statement
`P(S_closed, G) != P(S_open, G)` is ordinary state dependence, not a
contradiction. A **cryptic-site hypothesis** is stronger: the intended decision
depends on a pocket that is unavailable or materially changed in a specified
reference state but appears in one or more alternative states. It still needs a
defined alternative-state source and an appropriate experiment before it becomes
a binding or mechanism claim.

This state language prevents two common errors. An apo pocket is not automatically
the pocket used by a ligand-bound complex, and a predicted alternative does not
show that an unobserved ligand will stabilize it. Coordinates, population,
kinetics, ligandability, and functional consequence are distinct questions.

## Confidence asks about an output, not a molecular decision

Modern structure systems can provide confidence quantities alongside coordinates.
For example, the AlphaFold 3 paper describes `pLDDT` as predicted local accuracy
and `PAE` and `PDE` matrices as predicted errors for a generated structure
[Abramson et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07487-w). These
quantities can help identify portions of a proposed model that deserve cautious
interpretation. They do not measure an experimental population, a ligand
occupancy, an assay response, or the probability that a proposed pocket is
actionable.

Use confidence at the level it names. Local accuracy can guide cautious
side-chain annotation, while pairwise error can flag relative-placement
uncertainty. Neither makes model samples independent experimental structures;
a global score is weak where a pocket depends on domain motion.

| Question                                                    | Minimum object to retain                                                                     | What a confidence value cannot replace      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Is this a particular geometric pocket?                      | state `S`, rule `G`, coordinates and residue/volume output                                   | the definition of `G`                       |
| Does the open state plausibly differ from the closed state? | both coordinate sets, alignment rule, change summary                                         | an experimental state population            |
| May this site guide a next hypothesis?                      | target construct, intended decision, confidence and uncertainty notes                        | a binder, potency, or mechanism measurement |
| Can another reader reuse the asset?                         | stable identifier, source URL, assembly/chain, method, retrieval date and transformation log | undocumented coordinate editing             |

## Open structural assets are data, not illustrations

An open record is reusable only when a reader can identify what was retrieved.
The RCSB PDB programmatic interface exposes different services for search, data,
and coordinate access; those services do not erase the distinction between an
entry's metadata and a local derivative
[RCSB PDB Web APIs](https://www.rcsb.org/docs/programmatic-access/web-apis-overview).
For a teaching figure or downstream calculation, preserve the entry identifier,
assembly versus asymmetric-unit choice, chain or entity identifiers, experimental
method if supplied, resolution or validation fields when applicable, source URL,
retrieval date, and every coordinate transformation.

Do not silently repair missing atoms, rename residues, discard alternate
locations, or merge chains and then call the result “the PDB structure.” Such a
derivative can be useful, but it must name the edit and the purpose. Likewise,
a model prediction may be an open asset with a clear license or access route,
but it is not interchangeable with an experimental deposition. Reuse includes
its provenance and limitations, not just its coordinates.

Workflow openness has separate gates for code, weights, input schema, output
artifact, and licence. An open coordinate entry does not supply the others;
public code without declared inputs, outputs, and permission cannot establish
reproduction of a claimed workflow.

## Worked case: preserve two state hypotheses

All values in this case are **constructed for teaching**. A team holds two
coordinate assets for the same declared protein construct: `S_closed` and
`S_open`. Rule `G` calls a connected cavity `>= 150 Å³`; `S_closed` has `90 Å³`
near a selected residue set, while `S_open` has `160 Å³`. The two assets differ
by `2.5 Å` after a declared local alignment. A structure model reports
`pLDDT = 88` on its `0–100` scale around the putative gate and gate-to-domain
`PAE = 7.4 Å`; neither quantity is a probability of a cryptic site or binding.
An archive record gives `1.8 Å` as a reported experimental resolution for only
`S_closed`.

The valid teaching decision is not “a cryptic pocket is proven.” The team can
write: “Under `G`, the two declared coordinate assets yield different pocket
calls; the open-state geometry is a hypothesis whose relative placement remains
uncertain.” It should not copy the `1.8 Å` field to `S_open`, or replace an
uncertainty summary with a ligand-binding claim. A pose-sensitive follow-up must
ask whether the alternative geometry corresponds to the relevant target state.

## Limits and independent exercise

Pocket callers vary in solvent, probe-radius, side-chain, and connectivity
treatment. Predictors may generate alternatives where data do not constrain
them, and archive identifiers alone do not name the matching assembly, chain,
processing step, or biological condition. Narrow the decision rather than
discarding coordinates.

**Handworked exercise (not runnable or empirical):** A constructed project has
`S_A` and `S_B`. Under the same `G`, `P(S_A, G)` is a `90 Å³` cavity and
`P(S_B, G)` is a `210 Å³` cavity; the local alignment displacement is `1.0 Å`.
The `S_B` model output has `pLDDT = 91` on its `0–100` scale, but no experiment
has measured either state. Write one justified pocket statement and one statement
you must not make. Then list two metadata fields needed before sharing `S_B` as
a reusable asset.

<details data-worked-answer>
<summary>Worked answer</summary>

A justified statement is: “Under the declared rule `G`, the constructed
coordinates give different pocket volumes for `S_A` and `S_B`; `S_B` supplies a
hypothesis for an alternative pocket.” Do not state that the `210 Å³` pocket is
observed in a population, binds a ligand, or is druggable. Useful metadata
include a stable source identifier or URL, assembly and chain selection,
coordinate/model version, retrieval date, biological condition, and a log of
any transformation. `pLDDT = 91` is predicted local accuracy, not a probability
and not a replacement for the absent experiment.

</details>

## Read three deposited states yourself

The [EGFR structure evidence lab](/labs/egfr-structure-evidence/) runs this lesson's
argument on three deposited crystal structures of one kinase. The same erlotinib
ligand sits in one structure with a Lys745 to Glu762 distance of 3.71 A and in
another, which its depositors call inactive, at 14.41 A. The binding site grows from
15 to 25 residues as its cutoff moves from 4 to 6 A, and no file carries a ligand
outside the ATP site, so none of them can define a second pocket.
