## Decision map

Each row is a decision unit. The AI task is an input to that decision, not a
replacement for the required experimental feedback. A failure gate is a reason
to pause, revise, or stop rather than tune a score until it looks favorable.

| Decision unit        | Required evidence                                                                                                              | AI tasks that may help                                                                    | Experimental feedback                                                              | Failure gate                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Disease mechanism    | Defined indication, relevant biological context, and a traceable mechanism hypothesis                                          | Literature retrieval, causal or pathway prioritization                                    | Perturbation, biomarker, or disease-model result that bears on the mechanism       | No linked evidence supports a mechanism distinct from correlation                                       |
| Target hypothesis    | Target identity, disease link, tractability assumptions, and counterevidence                                                   | Knowledge-graph ranking, protein representation, target prioritization                    | Genetic, chemical, or functional perturbation in a relevant system                 | Target effect is not reproduced or conflicts with the intended therapeutic direction                    |
| Assay-ready target   | Construct, target state, assay endpoint, controls, and expected decision                                                       | Structure or pocket hypothesis, assay-condition data checks                               | Assay qualification and control outcomes                                           | Assay cannot distinguish the proposed mechanism or has unacceptable controls                            |
| Hit discovery        | Candidate pool, target state, screening endpoint, decoy or negative-control design, and chemical constraints                   | Similarity search, docking, pose prediction, virtual screening                            | Orthogonal binding or functional follow-up on selected candidates                  | No confirmed activity at the defined confirmation threshold or artifacts dominate                       |
| Hit-to-lead          | Confirmed series, structure-activity evidence, selectivity panel, and synthesizability constraints                             | Property prediction, structure-conditioned design, retrosynthesis                         | Repeated potency and selectivity measurements across an interpretable series       | No coherent series-level relationship or the route cannot meet the project constraints                  |
| Lead optimization    | Exposure and safety hypotheses, chemical series, target and off-target evidence, and a fixed objective                         | Multi-objective search, ADMET prediction, free-energy or dynamics calculations            | DMPK, safety, selectivity, and efficacy-relevant measurements                      | Improvement on one score degrades a decision-critical measured property without an acceptable trade-off |
| DMPK and toxicology  | Exposure, metabolism, pharmacology, safety, formulation, and species/context assumptions                                       | ADMET prediction, PK modelling, property prediction, and uncertainty-aware prioritization | Matched in vitro and in vivo DMPK or safety observations under the stated protocol | Exposure or safety evidence cannot support the intended dose, route, or therapeutic window              |
| Candidate nomination | A versioned package of mechanism, target, activity, selectivity, DMPK, safety, synthesis, uncertainty, and governance evidence | Evidence synthesis, traceability checks, constrained candidate ranking                    | Independent decision-review experiment or study specified before nomination        | Any required evidence stream is absent, contradicted, unqualified, or outside the stated authority      |

## Operating rules

1. Freeze the decision, comparator, and failure gate before observing a
   candidate model's preferred result.
2. Keep model inputs, evidence sources, predictions, and experimental outcomes
   connected to the same candidate and target state.
3. Preserve negative results and counterevidence in the candidate package.
4. Escalate only when experimental feedback changes the decision; a benchmark
   score alone is not a nomination receipt.
