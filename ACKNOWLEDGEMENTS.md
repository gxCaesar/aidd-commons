# Acknowledgements

AIDD Commons builds on a substantial open education, software, data, and scientific literature ecosystem. The project particularly acknowledges:

- TeachOpenCADD for demonstrating a modular, notebook-based route through computer-aided drug design;
- DeepChem for broad open tutorials and software spanning molecular machine learning;
- Therapeutics Data Commons for task-oriented datasets, splits, metrics, and benchmark infrastructure;
- ChEMBL and the European Bioinformatics Institute for maintaining versioned,
  programmatically accessible bioactivity records, release documentation, and
  explicit database terms used by the EGFR representation lab;
- RDKit contributors for the open cheminformatics implementation used to parse
  structures, calculate descriptors and fingerprints, and construct scaffolds;
- the European AI-driven Drug Discovery learning materials and Open & On-demand Drug Discovery Collaborative for complementary training and community models;
- Marin and Open Athena for articulating open development as the publication of process knowledge, including decisions, experiments, failures, and infrastructure;
- maintainers of public scientific databases, standards, benchmarks, cheminformatics tools, simulation engines, and biomolecular models represented in the Atlas;
- authors, curators, research participants, and experimental scientists whose work makes computational training and evaluation possible.

Acknowledgement is not a claim that these projects endorse AIDD Commons, nor does it replace resource-specific citation. Every lesson, lab, and report remains responsible for citing the particular source, dataset, model, or software it uses.

Contributors are credited through version history, release notes, authored content, and the citation metadata when appropriate. Curatorial work, correction, accessibility review, translation, replication, and negative-result reporting are treated as substantive contributions.
