# Third-party notices

AIDD Commons indexes and discusses third-party papers, datasets, models, software, standards, and tutorials. Listing does not transfer ownership, grant new rights, or imply endorsement. Each resource remains governed by the terms of its publisher and by applicable law.

## What the project redistributes

The public release contains original AIDD Commons code, prose, figures, tests, small synthetic fixtures, and reviewed experimental summaries. It does not intentionally redistribute third-party model weights or raw benchmark data. Labs provide upstream acquisition instructions unless redistribution has been explicitly reviewed and allowed.

Original software is under Apache-2.0. Original prose and figures are under CC BY 4.0. These licences do not cover quoted text, trademarks, external screenshots, paper figures, datasets, model parameters, or software owned by others.

## How terms are recorded

Atlas records distinguish code, weights, training data, data recipes, and other assets. Licence scope is stored beside the asset and linked to direct evidence. A blank or `unverified` field means that this project does not make a licence claim. It does not mean that an asset is free of restrictions.

The current registry includes upstream resources such as FreeSolv, RDKit, TeachOpenCADD, DeepChem, Therapeutics Data Commons, AlphaFold, OpenFold, Chai, Boltz, Protenix, OpenDDE, and many others. The canonical registry record, rather than this summary, is the maintained location for source URLs, asset scope, caveats, and last review date.

## Attribution and corrections

Lessons and reports cite the primary paper or official project page relevant to each claim. Reused material receives adjacent credit when required. If you own a listed resource and believe its description, terms, or attribution is wrong, use the correction form. Maintainers will narrow or remove disputed reuse while the evidence is reviewed.
