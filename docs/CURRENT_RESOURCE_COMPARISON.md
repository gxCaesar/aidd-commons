# Current product observations

Review date: 25 October 2026.

This review examines learner-facing capabilities on official public surfaces.
It supplements the historical 13-provider, 93-topic coverage comparison, whose
external observations remain frozen on 11 September 2026. It neither changes
those scores nor establishes current global leadership or learning outcomes.

## Scope

Four existing comparators received current product inspection: TeachOpenCADD,
DeepChem, O3DC and Therapeutics Data Commons. Two additional candidates, DMol
and ChemAI Atlas, received substantive inspection. An NPTEL course remains a
partial-access lead. Eight other external providers in the historical cohort
were not re-surveyed. This is a purposive product sample, not a worldwide census.

The review opened official websites, documentation, repositories and selected
lessons. No competitor notebook was executed, account created, dataset
downloaded or model evaluated. A documented launch or example is distinguished
from an observed successful run. Community surfaces do not establish response
quality, retention or educational benefit.

was insufficiently retrieved for substantive syllabus or assessment inspection.
Its teaching quality and access conditions remain unverified in this review.
Retrieval failure is not evidence that the course lacks those capabilities.

## Implications for AIDD Commons

The review motivates shorter, version-specific notebook launch routes, a
usable question/contribution entry, clearer task-to-lesson paths and explicit
source-maintenance dates. These are design judgments rather than measured
improvements in learning. AIDD Commons already has onboarding, worked answers,
personal notebook exercises and source-linked lessons, so the task is to make
the transitions easier to use and then observe actual readers.

Assess future changes against concrete tasks: selecting a route, opening and
retaining a personal exercise, interpreting a result and reaching the right
question or correction context. Preserve initial answers, assistance and
failures. A new scored comparison requires a declared cohort and current
item-level evidence under a common taxonomy. Counts from textbooks, toolkits,
benchmark indices and community services cannot be pooled into one ranking.
