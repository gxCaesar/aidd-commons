# Current product observations

Review date: 25 October 2026.

This qualitative review examines learner-facing capabilities on official public
surfaces. It supplements the historical 13-provider, 93-topic comparison, whose
external observations remain frozen on 11 September 2026. It does not change
those scores or establish current global leadership or learning outcomes.

## Scope

Current public surfaces were inspected for all twelve external identities in the
historical comparison. At least one substantive teaching or exercise artifact
was read for eleven of them. ML4Drug remains a proposal-only public-surface
observation. Two additional resources, DMol and ChemAI Atlas, received
substantive inspection. A further NPTEL course remains a partial-access lead.
This is a purposive product review, not a worldwide census or equally deep
review of every provider.

The review used official websites, documentation, repositories and selected
lessons. No competitor notebook was executed, account created, dataset
downloaded or model evaluated. A documented launch or saved output is distinct
from an observed fresh run. Access failures remain item-specific unknowns. A
community surface or one exchange does not establish response rates, retention
or educational benefit.

## Implications for AIDD Commons

AIDD Commons already supplies onboarding, worked and transfer questions, four
bilingual personal-notebook routes, source-pinned Colab links, a written capstone
and seven question/contribution forms. Prepared interfaces and local execution
are available artifacts. They do not establish successful hosted execution,
independent understanding or an operating exchange community.

The strongest remaining evidence need is actual unassisted interpretation and
transfer under the existing formative reader procedure. Record initial answers,
assistance, failures and the exact material used. A specialist editable SAR or
physical-simulation task and a learner-owned cross-unit case are useful product
directions when a concrete learning need justifies them. They should preserve
scientific identity, a competent comparison, stated inputs and a reviewable
interpretation. More notebooks alone would not close the evidence gap.

A useful scientific question, source-backed answer and resulting content repair
would demonstrate a feedback loop. Do not manufacture that activity. Real Colab
execution also needs an actual runtime observation before portability is
advertised. These outcomes require maintainers, readers and runtime operators.

A new scored comparison requires a declared cohort and current item-level
evidence under a common taxonomy. Counts from textbooks, toolkits, benchmark
indices and community services cannot be pooled into one ranking.
