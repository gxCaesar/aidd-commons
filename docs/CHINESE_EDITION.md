## Reader routes

- Use `/zh-cn/learn/foundations/resource-map/` for current unit-to-resource
  associations and target-evidence, ADMET/PK, biologics, physical-modelling and
  generation and scientific-agent resource guides. Atlas
  entries remain English but now link to their registered Chinese teaching use.

- Use `/zh-cn/learn/foundations/curriculum-map/` for the core and five specialist
  routes, unit objectives and available capabilities. A chapter link indicates
  written content, not completion of every possible research workflow in that unit.
- Follow records → baseline fitting → regression/classification → student sandbox
  for the first complete modelling line. The runnable example uses invented data;
  its English/Chinese reference numbers are the same actual local output.

- In the small-molecule route, use the assay and PK/PD chapters to connect model
  targets to measurement and exposure. The targets/cells route adds fine mapping,
  colocalization and MR reasoning. These are worked readings, not executable
  biological experiments or clinical guidance.

- Start at `/zh-cn/learn/foundations/first-session/`.
- Researchers can continue to `/zh-cn/learn/evaluation-and-reproducibility/assay-shift-diagnosis/`
  and `/zh-cn/learn/candidate-nomination/evidence-to-validation/` for a diagnostic
  memo and a proposed validation decision. Existing empirical results and
  invented measurement examples remain explicitly separate.
- Use `/zh-cn/pathways/` to select among the 18 tracks. Existing English links
  remain available alongside Chinese lesson links.
- Use `/zh-cn/labs/notebook-guide/` for the four-stage practice route, including
  the default offline PaperQA source-inspection exercise,
  cell-state example, saving instructions and troubleshooting. `/zh-cn/labs/`
  also links the 56 translated lab guides and curated downloads.
- The language selector on a lesson switches to the corresponding page.
  Every translated lesson or lab also links its canonical English original.
- The T17 introduction links to `/zh-cn/learn/evaluation-and-reproducibility/split-and-leakage-cases/`
  for four reading cases and expandable answers based on completed labs.
- Continue at `/zh-cn/learn/evaluation-and-reproducibility/metrics-and-uncertainty/`
  for four cases on error, ranks, prediction intervals and budgeted recovery.
- The T15 bridge at `/zh-cn/learn/active-learning/diversity-uncertainty-feedback/`
  uses four cases to separate diversity selection, predictive assessment and
  outcome-informed acquisition.

Lesson completion uses the same lesson identity and browser-local storage in
both languages. Completing a Chinese lesson also marks its English version
complete in that browser. This is optional local progress, not an account,
cross-device synchronization, or evidence of learning.

## Scientific and language boundaries

English remains canonical. Translations preserve the source dates, quantities,
equations, commands, source URLs, uncertainty and negative conclusions. Their
translation dates do not imply a new source review or experiment run.

Scientific result panels and interactive diagrams keep their canonical English
labels and shared numerical inputs. Molecular representation inspection, synthetic
modelling, lipophilicity and PaperQA each provide an English/Chinese notebook pair
with identical executable cells. Lipophilicity has one completed local real-data
run; both offline PaperQA editions executed locally. The separate PaperQA batch
completed. The first CPU-only live attempt timed out without an answer; a later
English execution copy with four model threads and longer timeouts completed
one question. This does not validate the unchanged default CPU limits, a Chinese
live run or overall answer quality. Actual
Colab execution remains unverified for all four pairs. Historical downloaded notebooks,
protocols, examples and summaries retain their original content. Chinese prose explains how to
read them; it does not maintain a separate translated numerical dataset.

The Atlas, comparison audit, report and contribution pages remain English.
Chinese navigation labels these destinations accordingly. Automatic fallback
content is not counted as a completed translation.

The completed T05, T11 and T15 outcomes and frozen protocols are unchanged.
In particular, the Chinese edition retains the ChEMBL representation ranking
reversal, shared-label exposure, negative MaxMin results and missing per-frame
Ridge prediction record. It does not claim independent full recomputation,
prospective discovery, or uniform model superiority.
