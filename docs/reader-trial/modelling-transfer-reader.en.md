# Modelling transfer task sheet

This session is a small separate session. It is not extra required work in the
30-minute orientation. It checks whether the material supports a modelling
decision, not whether you are right. Nothing you write here is marked right or
wrong. You may skip any part or stop at any point.

Do not provide names, account details, credentials or private data.

## What you are being asked

Follow the core route with the site as you normally would. Record the
navigation separately from the questions that follow. Say when you open a
worked answer or receive help, and keep your first explanation before you
revise it.

## What will be recorded

For every task the organizer records what you did, the kind of help you needed
and the time you spent. Three kinds of help are recorded separately.

- A page pointer. Someone told you where to look and you read it yourself.
- A concept explained. Someone explained the reasoning to you.
- The answer. Someone gave you the answer.

The record is not scored, and it is not used to judge you.

## Where to start

Open `/learn/foundations/curriculum-map/#core-route` and follow the core route
through records, fitting, regression or classification, and the sandbox.

## Task 1. Follow the core route

Reach the records lesson, the fitting lesson, the regression and
classification lesson and the sandbox. Write down the path you took and where
you had to guess.

## Task 2. Apply the aggregation rule

A new export gives parent P exact IC50 values 20 nM and 200 nM, plus parent Q
with IC50 greater than 2 µM. Apply the lesson's log-then-median rule. Explain
which record does not enter an exact-value regression.

## Task 3. Read a classifier threshold

A trained classifier gives probabilities [0.9, 0.6, 0.4] for recorded classes
[0, 1, 1]. Which mistakes occur at threshold 0.5, and can the test records be
used to pick a new threshold while retaining an untouched-test claim?

## Task 4. Optional sandbox execution

Run only the synthetic student sandbox in a reader copy, locate its output,
and explain why test MAE 4.5 is worse than the constant baseline's 3.5
despite zero validation MAE. Do not run a frozen assay evaluation.
