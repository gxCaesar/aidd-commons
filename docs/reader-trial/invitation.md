# Invitation to a first-reader session

Thank you for offering to look at our teaching material. This session is a
formative check of whether the material works for someone meeting it for the
first time. You are evaluating the material. You are not being tested.

## Who the session is for

We want readers with different backgrounds. A student of chemistry or biology,
a researcher who works with machine learning, and an engineer who is
comfortable in a terminal are each useful, and none of them is required. We
also want readers who are willing to say where the material lost them.

## What the session asks of you

The main part takes about 30 minutes. You can do it in English or in Chinese,
whichever you prefer to read. You will use the material the way an ordinary
reader would. We will ask you to say where you looked, what you expected to
find there, and where the material stopped you getting what you needed.

Some sessions include one optional step that runs a small example on your own
computer. That step carries its own 15-minute limit and you can skip it
without changing the rest of the session.

There is no score, no pass mark and no ranking. Nobody is compared with
another reader, and no session result is published under your name. We are not
measuring what you know about chemistry, biology or machine learning. We are
measuring whether the instructions say clearly enough what to do and what the
result means.

## What gets written down

The facilitator writes down the page or exercise you reached, the first point
where you were unsure, your own explanation before any help was given, whether
you opened a worked answer or received a hint, the time the session took, and
which mode you used. Everything is filed under an anonymous label that the
facilitator assigns.

We do not collect names, email addresses, employer details, credentials,
private datasets, or screenshots that contain personal information. Your notes
stay with the facilitator. We publish only a correction that you have seen and
agreed to, with anything identifying removed.

## What you can decide

You may stop at any point. You do not have to give a reason, and a session
that ends early is still a useful session. A partial answer is a valid
observation.

You may skip any question. You may ask for a question to be repeated, and you
may ask to move on to something else.

## The material before you agree

The material you would evaluate is attached to this invitation or sent with
it. Please read it before you decide whether to take part. If it did not
arrive, ask the organizer to send it.

## How to decline

Reply to the message that invited you, or tell the organizer in any way you
prefer. You do not need to give a reason. Declining changes nothing else, and
you can still be offered a session later if you change your mind.

## What to prepare

Nothing at all. You do not need an account, a dataset, a model, a graphics
card, or any prior preparation. The optional local step needs a terminal and
Python 3.11 or 3.12, and we can tell you before the session whether it is
worth attempting on your machine.
